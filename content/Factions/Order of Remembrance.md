*“Life is a Gift, Death is a Promise.”*

Order in service to the Raven Queen, As the Order is Lawful Neutral that's focus is on balance of life and death. The more a place prospers the larger the harvest for the Raven Queen.
- Sect of the Plow
	- Consists of Order members that go out adventuring to clear areas of monsters and bandits. This is so new villages and farmland can be claimed. Usually Clerics and Paladins of the order, may be accompanied by other adventures outside the order.
- Sect of the Sower
	- Sects purpose is to support frontier villages and farmers in claiming and cultivating the land. Usually Clerics, Priests (Warlocks) and Librarians of the order. Sect of the Plow support removing any threats to the frontier.
- Sect of The Cultivator
	- Sects focus is to help grow established areas, long standing villages or growing towns. Once the land is more established the focus becomes more about supporting the people and providing security. Paladins and Cleric would build a barracks as a base of operations and recruitment of new members of the Order.
- Sect of the Harvester
	- Sect focused on the care of the people. Normally Clerics and Priests provide care or last rights to the population.

**Order Secrets**
There are members of the order that are tasked with ensuring the cultivation of cities/towns/villages. Their purpose when required is to remove the “Weeds” or “Prune” the vine. When the time comes they will enter a location and remove any corrupting or unwanted elements.

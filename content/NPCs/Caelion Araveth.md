#NPC [[Human]]  
**Appearance**  
Man in White robes with gold trim, White bread and Hair.  
Emits golden energy.  
Around his head is a golden light.  
Seem to be focused on an orb in the Gateway Chamber.

**Demeaner**  
Seems to a man of good humour. Almost fatherly in his tone.  
Humble in his requests for help.  

Does not claim to be the master of the tower. Is one of its occupants and the summoner of the Heroes.  

**Motivation**
Wants to save Reality from collapse.  
Brings heroes to the tower to re-imprison [[Azreal]] corruptor of realities.
